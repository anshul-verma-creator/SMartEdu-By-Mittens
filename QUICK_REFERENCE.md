## 🔄 Common Workflows

### Workflow 1: New Student Admission (5 mins)
1. Click "Admissions"
2. Fill admission form
3. Submit application
4. Officer reviews and approves
5. Student record created automatically

### Workflow 2: Collect Student Fee (3 mins)
1. Click "Fee Collection"
2. Select student
3. Enter amount & date
4. Choose payment method
5. System generates receipt

### Workflow 3: Allocate Hostel Room (3 mins)
1. Click "Hostel Management"
2. Select student
3. Choose block & room
4. Set bed number
5. Allocation complete

### Workflow 4: Record Exam Result (2 mins)
1. Click "Examination Records"
2. Select student
3. Enter subject & marks
4. Set exam date
5. Result saved automatically
